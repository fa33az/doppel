# Code of Conduct

doppel is maintained by people who give their time for free, and used by
people who are often having a very bad day (their protocol just got drained).
Be kind to both.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially in reviews.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone had a first PR once.
- Accept that "no" is sometimes the answer, and give reasons when you say it.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Posting details of an unpatched vulnerability in a live contract in public
  issues or discussions. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to handle that.
- Using the project's spaces to recruit for, or coordinate, attacks on
  contracts.

## Scope

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
you represent the project.

## Enforcement

If something here is being broken, contact the maintainer
([@fa33az](https://github.com/fa33az)). If you'd rather not raise it in
public, open a minimal issue asking for a private channel. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or ban people from the
project, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
